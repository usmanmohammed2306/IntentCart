A decision worth studying.

This instrument supports an operator-reviewed, consented pilot. Instrument readiness does not establish human validation.

Before you start

Adults 18+ only. Compare fictional backpacks within a $90 total budget and 14-inch laptop fit. Expect up to 16 comparisons and a short survey, about 5–10 minutes. A policy is assigned at random. Recommendations stay hidden during independent comparison phases.

With your consent, this computer stores an anonymous study ID, assignment, choices, timestamps and survey answers. No name, email, payment or account credentials are requested. Use the withdrawal button to delete this record. The operator should delete raw records within 30 days; no automatic retention purge is implemented. Participation is voluntary; you may stop at any time. This is a usability pilot, not a validated efficacy study.
