IntentCart is an experimental hackathon application for workstation planning and customer organization, operated by the project owner. It is not a real-money storefront.

What is stored

Your username, display name, password hash, hashed recovery key, account security events, plans, visual layouts, uploaded documents and customer records are stored on this deployment’s persistent volume. Cookies keep you signed in and protect forms; no advertising cookies are used. Passwords and recovery keys are never stored as plain text.

Who can access it

Other accounts cannot open your plans, files or customer records. A designated application operator can manage account status and AI permission. Like any self-hosted service, the infrastructure owner can access storage and backups; this is not end-to-end encryption. Contact details are entered by you and are not verified. Do not upload sensitive, regulated or third-party confidential information.

Shared IT teams

IT team membership shares that team’s inventory, supplier quotes, employee equipment requests, approval decisions and activity with its members. The team owner can add existing accounts and remove members. Review the exact username and role before granting access. Store a minimal employee label, not HR, health, financial or other regulated records. Procurement checks run locally without sending this team data to a model or supplier. Downloaded handoffs and historical records are your responsibility to keep private. An external order or receiving entry is a team member’s report, not independent supplier or payment verification.

AI and external services

The main workspace uses a task-trained Qwen language model running on this application server. Requests and observed-state features are processed here; account credentials and customer notes are not model inputs. Its training dataset contains authored synthetic examples, not your account data. Scene gestures and account/customer actions send no paid model request.

Astropods provides hosting. Optional sponsor-model comparisons send your entered request and observed catalog evidence to the Astropods gateway only when you choose that mode. File excerpts are sent only after the file-specific sharing checkbox and Ask AI action. Sponsor access requires an operator invitation and a fixed allowance; account creation does not replenish it. No real-money PayPal payment is enabled.

Recovery, retention and control

Keep the one-time recovery key shown when you create your account or change your password. There is no email verification or reset email service. Losing both password and recovery key can prevent recovery. Sessions expire after seven days or one day of inactivity; you can sign out all devices at once. Account records and customer notes persist until removed by the operator. Archived customers remain stored. Deactivation prevents access and retains records; it is not deletion. Ask the project operator for erasure or restoration through the project’s GitHub profile, without posting private data in a public issue.
