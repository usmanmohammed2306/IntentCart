## Current 3.0 robustness study

See `ROBUSTNESS.md`: development 140 personas/420 sessions, held-out 280 independent personas/seven known families, nine policies/2,520 sessions plus 2,520 sensitivity sessions. Primary recency reduction −0.002153, 95% CI [−0.005630, 0.001035]; no pooled improvement. Early stopping trades quality/coverage for fewer questions. Controller remains opt-in. Reproduce the bundled benchmark with:

```sh
python scripts/evaluate.py --personas 120 --seed 2026
```

The script evaluates identical synthetic users under intake-only, random, maximum-answer-uncertainty, and decision-aware policies. It uses a shared catalog, constraints, prior, and common random draws per question step. It reports utility regret, top-pick agreement, held-out pair agreement, 1,000-resample bootstrap intervals, paired regret differences, and measured query latency. The complete machine-readable output is `intentcart/data/evaluation.json`; `/evidence` renders it.

The 120-profile seed-2026 run found:

| Policy | Mean utility regret | Top-pick agreement | Questions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Intake only | 0.018396 | 37.5% | 0 |
| Random comparisons | 0.016341 | 42.5% | 4 |
| Maximum answer uncertainty | 0.015824 | 45.8% | 4 |
| Decision-aware | 0.016348 | 42.5% | 4 |

The decision-aware versus random paired regret difference is −0.000007, with a 95% bootstrap interval of [−0.003280, 0.003313]. **This run does not establish superiority of decision-aware questioning.** The experiment is included to expose that fact, not to hide it. It is evidence that the implementation is measurable and comparable; it is not evidence of human conversion lift or ROI.

Hidden users have Dirichlet(1.4) nonnegative linear weights. Their responses follow the same logistic family used by the learner (beta=12). A weak intake prior is derived from their hidden top two attributes. Budgets are $80/$90/$115 total and laptop constraints 14/16 inches. This is favorable model-family alignment, though the intake-conditioned prior is only heuristic. Results do not generalize to arbitrary nonlinear preferences, inaccurate specifications, social effects, changing goals, or real shopping behavior. Latency reflects this development machine and may differ elsewhere.
