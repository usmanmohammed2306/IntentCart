3. `decision-validity.png` — The new Decision lab separates four claims: does the plan work, is it the preferred choice, may it be purchased, and did payment complete? Compare five policies over ten scenario structures, one hundred twenty two possible worlds and five budgets. These are three thousand fifty correlated simulation rows, not independent trials. Budget exhaustion stays distinct from proving no solution exists.

5. `decision-replay.png` — Payment reliability is executable. Real application endpoints run against an independent fake provider ledger, across interpreter restarts and injected failures. Download the trace and replay it locally. No real payment or model request runs.

Configured checkout requires explicit quote approval and separate PayPal sandbox buyer approval. A plan certificate cannot claim a completed payment.

7. `sandbox-refund.jpg` — Separate historical evidence shows an actual sixty two dollar PayPal sandbox capture and full refund from October seventh. Current failure experiments use a fake provider, with clearly separate records. No goods are sold or shipped.

8. `missions-mobile.jpg` — IntentCart includes the responsive workspace, reproducible experiments, source identities and free local judge instructions. One hundred ninety Python tests and twenty one JavaScript tests pass. Optional model interpretation stays disabled and live inference unverified. The result is a complete local demonstration with inspectable evidence and deliberate payment boundaries.
